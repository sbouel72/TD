The Series

A Manifesto for the Un-Homed

The Bridge Walker Series is a raw, unflinching reckoning with what it means to be displaced at the very root of your being — and the fierce, sacred work of building sovereignty from the ruins of severance.

Born from Shane Bouel's own experience as an adoptee, this four-part series moves through the inner landscape of exile, the machinery of societal forgetting, and the defiant architecture of return. It is part memoir, part manifesto, part spiritual cartography.

These are not books about being rescued. They are books about becoming the architect of your own ground — about transforming the sacred fracture of displacement into the most powerful source of self-knowledge and cosmic sovereignty.

An Excerpt

The Cry in the Silence

Prologue — The Sacred Fracture, A Manifesto for the Un-Homed

It was not a memory I could speak, but a tremor in the bone, a ripple across the nerve-net. Before language, before even light fully registered, there was the tear. Not of flesh, not directly, but a severing in the field of being, a primal sundering.

This initial fracture became the indelible blueprint, etched into the very clay of my making. It marked the precise coordinates of my un-homing. A perpetual slight misalignment, like a bone set badly, always reminding itself of its original break.

But within this profound and constant tremor, within the cry that never ceased to sound in silence, a strange potential began to stir. The fracture, while a source of unrelenting pain, also became a unique aperture. Where the world saw a void, a missing piece, I sensed a raw, exposed nerve, keenly attuned to frequencies others ignored.

About the Author

Shane Bouel

Shane Bouel is an Australian author, adoptee, and sovereign voice in the discourse surrounding adoption, identity, and spiritual self-determination. Writing from the interior landscape of lived displacement, Bouel brings together memoir, manifesto, and mystical inquiry in a body of work that refuses the easy comforts of the "lucky child" narrative.

The Bridge Walker Series is Shane's defiant declaration that the wound of severance, when honestly held and fiercely interrogated, becomes the most potent ground for self-knowledge. His work speaks to every adoptee, every displaced soul, every person who has been handed an origin story written by someone else's hand.
